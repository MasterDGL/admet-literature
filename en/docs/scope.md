The collection centers on [ADMET and pharmacokinetics](../topics/admet.md), supported by [methods and benchmarks](../topics/foundations.md). The [knowledge map](knowledge-map.md) connects the areas below and guides future expansion.

| Area | Research questions | Current coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ADMET and human pharmacokinetics | Absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, toxicity and cross-species PK | Dedicated topic; counts appear in the generated index |
| Molecular representations and property prediction | Graphs, SMILES, 3D representations, pretraining and transfer | Foundational methods and ADMET applications |
| Protein representations and structure | Sequence models, structure prediction and drug-discovery applications | Planned |
| Interactions, docking and virtual screening | Binding poses, affinity, scoring and screening reliability | Planned |
| Molecular generation and multiparameter optimization | Design constrained by activity, synthesis and ADMET | Planned |
| Reactions and synthesis routes | Reaction prediction, retrosynthesis and experimental feasibility | Planned |
| Data, benchmarks and experimental validation | Data quality, blind tests, distribution shifts and reproducibility | ADMET-related entries available; other areas planned |

New topics use the same paper-record structure, organizing methods, data and results around specific drug-discovery questions.
