## Experimental setup and analysis

The ADMET Benchmark Group uses task-specific metrics and scaffold splits, with evaluation across multiple random seeds.

Toxicity tasks among the 22 endpoints include hERG, AMES, DILI and LD50. TDC also hosts other drug-discovery data; select tasks by benchmark group.
