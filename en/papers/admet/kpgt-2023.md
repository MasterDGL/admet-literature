## Experimental setup and analysis

**Design.** Eleven standard property datasets use 8:1:1 scaffold splits and three runs; 22 TDC tasks use 7:1:2 scaffold splits and five runs. TDC comparisons include 28 classical and deep-learning baselines. Metrics are task-specific: AUROC/AUPRC for classification and MAE/Spearman r for regression.

| TDC task | Metric | KPGT | Best baseline listed in the paper |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caco-2, 906 molecules | MAE ↓ | 0.284±0.009 | BaseBoosting 0.285±0.005 |
| P-gp inhibition, 1,212 molecules | AUROC ↑ | 0.938±0.004 | ZairaChem 0.935±0.006 |
| CYP2C9 inhibition, 12,092 molecules | AUPRC ↑ | 0.797±0.006 | ZairaChem 0.786±0.004 |
| hERG, 648 molecules | AUROC ↑ | 0.847±0.024 | SimGCN 0.874±0.014 |
| AMES, 7,255 molecules | AUROC ↑ | 0.868±0.003 | ZairaChem 0.871±0.002 |

Source: Supplementary Table 8, mean±SD. The 16/22 count describes the paper’s historical comparison; hERG and AMES illustrate tasks where KPGT did not lead.

**Comparison scope.** The supplement lists PPBR size as 1,614 versus 1,797 in the TDC dictionary, and labels CYP3A4 substrate as AUPRC versus AUROC in the TDC protocol. These two entries await reconciliation before cross-method comparison; the other 20 retain their reported values.

Small differences in mean scores, such as Caco-2, should be interpreted alongside run variability. MoleculeACE and HPK1/FGFR1 experiments provide separate transfer evaluations.
