## Experimental setup and analysis

Evaluates TDC, MoleculeNet and MoleculeACE separately. Figure 2c and Supplementary Table 8 show the best compared results on 16 of 22 TDC tasks.

Pretraining uses approximately 2 million ChEMBL29 molecules; MoleculeACE further tests activity-cliff prediction. Ablations help assess knowledge-guided pretraining and downstream transfer.
