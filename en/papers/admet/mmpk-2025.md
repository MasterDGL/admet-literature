# MMPK: A Multimodal Deep Learning Framework to Predict Human Oral Pharmacokinetic Parameters

*Journal of Medicinal Chemistry · 2025-07-31* · Published · Further reading

**Overview:** Combines molecular graphs, substructure graphs, SMILES and dose to predict eight human oral pharmacokinetic parameters.

Topics: Human oral PK, Multimodal, Multitask learning, External validation.

## Experimental setup and analysis

**Design.** Code splits unique SMILES into ten folds, then reserves 1/9 of the remaining molecules for validation, giving roughly 8:1:1. Dose records for one molecule stay together. Lower GMFE is better.

| Endpoint | MMPK CV mean±SD | XGBoost CV mean±SD | Investigational external | 2024-approved external |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AUC | 3.929±0.589 | 4.163±0.535 | 4.140 | 3.215 |
| Cmax | 3.135±0.358 | 3.165±0.257 | 2.758 | 2.305 |
| Half-life | 2.468±0.261 | 2.405±0.241 | 2.262 | 2.753 |
| CL/F | 4.029±0.636 | 4.232±0.578 | 3.861 | 2.236 |
| Vz/F | 3.476±0.356 | 3.579±0.404 | 3.472 | 2.836 |

Sources: Tables S4, S8 and S9; external scores use averaged predictions. The raw external categories contain 118/9 compounds and 602/33 records, respectively; endpoint evaluation counts depend on available labels.

CL/F and Vz/F are apparent oral parameters. Endpoint sizes and errors vary; raw records, compound–dose combinations and unique compounds are separate counts.
